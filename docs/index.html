Honest about what is possible

Once a file is gone from every bin and cache, modern Android erases its space, and no app can bring it back without root. Unlose shows you exactly what is still there, where it was found, and how long the phone's trash will keep it.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking, and no internet access at all. Unlose never edits or deletes what it finds; it saves new copies you choose. Read the privacy policy.
