Where are my recovered files?

In your gallery's "Unlose" album (DCIM/Unlose). Audio goes to Music/Unlose and documents to Documents/Unlose.

Why does Unlose need "All files access"?

Deleted files wait in bins, caches and hidden folders that normal photo access cannot reach. Unlose reads them on your phone only; it has no internet access.

Quick scan or deep scan?

A quick scan checks bins, thumbnails and hidden copies, usually in under a minute. A deep scan also identifies unnamed files and rebuilds pictures from cache files, which takes longer.

Why can't it find a photo I deleted long ago?

Phones empty their trash after about 30 days, and modern Android erases the space of deleted files. Without root, no app can read that space. Also check your cloud backup's trash (for example Google Photos).

What do "Put back" and "Recover" do?

"Recover" saves a new copy into the Unlose album. "Put back" asks Android to restore an item from its own trash to where it was.
